Our story

Marietta Research Solutions was founded by Dr. James G. Maloney after 25+ years across academia, defense, intelligence, and commercial industry — including roles as Chief Scientist at Georgia Tech Research Institute and co-founder of the engineering practice at Compass Technology Group.

The thesis is simple: the most useful EM consulting is also the most direct. A two-person practice means the engineer who scopes your problem is the engineer who solves it. A contractor network means we can scale to a multi-year phased-array program when needed — without turning into a body shop.
