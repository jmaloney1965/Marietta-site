Featured · Worked Example

CC1101 Sub-GHz Matching Network

IoT radio · 902–928 MHz ISM · matching network design

Off-the-shelf TI CC1101 transceiver paired with a compact PCB antenna. Problem: as integrated, return loss was > −5 dB across the band — half the radiated power lost to mismatch. We designed a two-element matching network, fabricated a revised board, and measured < −15 dB return loss across the entire 26 MHz ISM band.
