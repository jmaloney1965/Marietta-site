What happens after you hit submit

We keep the path from first contact to signed SOW short and honest. Here's the shape of a typical engagement — the full version, with deliverables, IP terms, and pricing model, lives on the FAQ page.

1

Reply within one business day

Dr. Maloney reviews every inquiry personally. You'll get either a 30-minute scoping call (free, no obligation) or a candid "this isn't a fit, here's who you should talk to" referral. No sales funnel, no junior engineer triage.

2

Scoping call & (if needed) NDA

On the call we'll dig into the technical problem, constraints, frequency band, timeline, and platform. If the work involves sensitive information we'll execute a mutual NDA up front — ours or yours. See the FAQ entry on NDAs for our standard terms.

3

Written proposal & SOW

You'll receive a fixed-scope study, hourly consulting agreement, or multi-phase development proposal depending on what fits the work. Each option lists deliverables, timeline, price, and IP terms in plain language. See the FAQ for cost ranges and engagement types.

4

Kickoff & weekly status

Once the SOW is signed we begin work within one to two weeks, depending on current load. You get weekly status — progress, blockers, decisions needed — plus interim deliverables at agreed checkpoints. Final delivery includes a written technical report on every engagement.

SBIR / federal contracts: MRS is structured to participate as prime or subcontractor on SBIR/STTR efforts. NAICS codes available on request; CAGE code in process. Mention SBIR in the form above and we'll send full registration details on the scoping call.

Other common questions — IP ownership, deliverable formats, frequency-range coverage, fabrication and measurement, AI/ML capabilities — are answered on the FAQ page. If yours isn't there, ask in the form and we'll address it on the scoping call.
