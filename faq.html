Antenna & Engineering Questions

What antenna types do you design?

Fragmented aperture antennas, blade antennas, phased arrays, electrically small antennas, broadband and wideband designs, conformal antennas, matching networks, and platform-integrated systems. We work UHF through millimeter wave (roughly 100 MHz – 100 GHz). See our pages on Fragmented Aperture, Blade Antennas, and Antenna Integration.

What simulation tools do you use?

Our primary tool is a proprietary FDTD solver developed in-house over 25+ years and used by multiple defense primes. We also use commercial tools (HFSS, CST, FEKO) when client workflow requires it, and supplement with custom genetic-algorithm optimization, matching-network synthesis tools, and post-processing pipelines we've written.

Can you handle ITAR-controlled or classified work?

Yes for ITAR/EAR-controlled unclassified work — please confirm export status before any technical exchange. Classified work depends on facility and personnel clearance status; reach out to discuss specifics.

Do you build hardware, or only deliver designs?

We deliver designs, simulation reports, and integration packages. We do not manufacture antennas. We're vendor-neutral — recommendations are driven by what's best for your program.

Can you support test campaigns and prototype bring-up?

Yes — we provide written test plans, review measurement data, advise on tuning, and join range or chamber test campaigns when needed.

Process & Deliverables

How does the consultation process work?

A brief intro call to understand your goals and required deliverables. We agree on scope, timeline, and what you'll provide. Work is delivered in clear drafts with refinement opportunities. Most engagements are fixed-scope, fixed-fee; we use hourly billing only when scope can't be predicted.

What does a typical engagement look like — timeline and cost?

Antenna design engagements typically run 4–12 weeks depending on complexity, with milestones at requirements lock, simulation review, and final delivery. Feasibility studies are 2–4 weeks. Cost depends entirely on scope — we provide a written proposal after the intro call so there are no surprises.

What deliverables do I receive?

Standard deliverables include: written design report, simulation results (S-parameters, patterns, efficiency), manufacturable design package (geometry, materials, feed structure), integration drawings, and a measurement plan for prototype validation. The exact package is defined at scope agreement.

Can you guarantee outcomes (court results, funding, approvals)?

No. We help you prepare, organize, and present information clearly, but we cannot guarantee outcomes that depend on third parties, agencies, courts, or employers. We can guarantee that our engineering work meets the specifications agreed at scope.

Are you a patent attorney or patent agent?

No. Patent services are technical drafting and documentation support intended to help you prepare materials for review by a registered patent attorney or patent agent.

Legal, Confidentiality & ITAR

What about confidentiality?

Please don't send sensitive personal or proprietary data through the public website form. We treat all client designs as confidential and routinely operate under NDA. We can provide a mutual NDA template, or work under yours.

Will you sign our NDA?

Yes — most standard mutual NDAs are signed without modification. We may suggest minor edits for one-way agreements that conflict with our standard practice (publication of generic methods, etc.).

Who owns the IP from a consulting engagement?

Default: the client owns deliverables created specifically under the engagement. Pre-existing IP (our FDTD solver, patented technologies like Fragmented Aperture, our optimization tooling) remains ours. Custom IP terms — including assignments and licenses — are negotiable. This is always written into the engagement letter.

Do you provide PE-stamped drawings?

No. We do not provide professional engineer (PE) stamped plans or drawings unless explicitly contracted with a licensed PE as required by law.
