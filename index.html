CC1101 Sub-GHz Matching Network

IoT radio · 315–928 MHz ISM · 4 bands · matching network design

Off-the-shelf TI CC1101 transceiver paired with a compact PCB and two chip antennas. Client (KickR Design) needed all four ISM bands matched in-case, with display installed. We designed and tuned matching networks for each band and measured ≤ −10 dB return loss across every required range. Delivered in weeks.
